# Deepstack audit, 2026-11-07 (main at 60af469, after #324)

Playtest of the built game (`npm run build`, served from dist/) in headless Chromium with OS reduced motion on. Two windows: phone 375x667 (2x) and desktop 1280x800. For each of the seven rooms (Reading Room, Chronicle, Counting House, Observatory, Scriptorium, Gallery, Anatomy Theatre) it walks home, one Daily word and its result card, the Unlimited book, one Unlimited word and its result card, and the Rooms book. On desktop it also presses `]` to walk a door. Each step runs a check for sideways scroll, vertical scroll on home, scrollable panes, clipped text (the same detector as CI's `textFit`), and console or page errors.

Screenshots: `screenshots/audit/playtest-2026-11-07/` (115 files: `phone-<room>-<step>.png`, `desk-<room>-<step>.png`, two zoom crops, and `playtest-report.json`). The script is `notes/audit-playtest.mjs`. It needs a Chromium path (`CHROMIUM_PATH`) and an `OUT` folder.

Note: `screenshots/audit/` already held an earlier batch (`home-1280.png`, `pp-*.png` and so on). This run's `playtest-report.json` was first written to the folder root and then moved into the dated subfolder, so the root copy from the earlier batch, if there was one, has been replaced.

## What passed
- No page errors and no console errors in any of the 14 windows.
- No sideways scroll and no vertical scroll on home, at either size, in any room.
- No scrollable panes that trap content.
- Daily and Unlimited start and finish in every room. Each Daily is per-room, and Unlimited shows "Not yet placed" until placed.
- Phone home, every room: the tagline, fire colour and Daily line fit. The Room Ledger and Unlimited book fit.

## Confirmed defects (verified by eye and by geometry)

1. **Desktop home: the card catalogue and Librarian's Card are cropped at 1280x800.** Both cabinets are 266 px wide, but their content is 271 px (`scrollWidth` 271, `clientWidth` 266), so the outer edge is cut. In the zoom crop the left column of catalogue drawers has no left edge. The Librarian's Card paper runs to the frame with no right edge. Source: `src/ui/cabinet.css` (`.cc-cab`), `src/ui/welcome.css` (lines 16-17, `.cc-cab` and `.ledger`), `src/ui/scope.css`. Zoom crops: `playtest-2026-11-07/zoom-cabinet-1280.png` and `zoom-librarian-card-1280.png`.
2. **Librarian's Card, STREAK row: the room tag sits on the rule.** "READING ROOM" (or "GALLERY" and so on) sits under the STREAK label, and its dot is on the line. The DEEPEST and BOOKS rows are left-aligned while the STREAK label is centred, so the rows do not line up. Same crop as above.
3. **Result card: the "You wrote" echo is cut mid-sentence.** `.wrote` in `src/styles/result.css:24` clamps to two lines with `overflow: hidden`. A longer answer ends "...to try earnestly and.." on phone and desktop (seen on the Reading Room Daily, phone-hall-3 and desk-hall-3). Nate's rule is that words must read in full. This sits inside "answer/results flow", which is already open, so it is listed for that thread and not briefed here.
4. **Rooms: the count wording is inconsistent.** The Reading Room reads "16 words met" on the ledger while the other rooms read "0 of 413 met". This belongs to the open "copy pass" thread.
